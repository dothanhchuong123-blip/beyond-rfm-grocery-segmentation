# Executed analysis notes

- Actual Kaggle archive downloaded without authentication through the public dataset API; no fabricated or simulated observations. Raw inputs are intentionally ignored by Git. SHA-256 values and verified runtime versions are in provenance.json.
- 2,595,732 transaction lines and 92,353 products; no exact duplicate rows, missing cells in the used tables, duplicate product IDs, unmatched product links, or conflicting basket-household/day keys. Exclude the union of nonpositive quantity/sales: 18,917 lines; retain 2,576,815. Empty/whitespace department strings normalized to UNKNOWN. Department labels trimmed. Complete UTF-8/schema inventory for all eight downloaded CSV files is in schema_inventory.json; only two tables enter the analysis.
- Fixed calibration DAY<=500; holdout DAY501–711. 2,498 calibration households; two holdout-only households excluded from the fixed cohort. Keep all calibration households, including 59 with no later positive purchases.
- RFM: recency = 501 minus latest purchase DAY; frequency = distinct baskets; monetary = sum of recorded SALES_VALUE. Behavior adds mean basket sales, mean basket line count, unique product count, department count, recorded discount line fraction. Positive magnitude features transformed log1p and standardized. Feature counts3/8/17 for RFM/Behavior/Product. Product composition = top8 calibration departments + OTHER, square root shares, centered; category block total variance equals behavior block total variance. All preprocessing calibration-only.
- KMeans n_init20/random_state42 and Ward k2..8; DBSCAN min_samples10, eps calibrated to .5/.7/.85/.95 quantiles of 10-neighbor distances. Silhouette evaluated on fixed sample up to1500, seed42; DBSCAN excludes noise when computing valid-cluster metrics, but noise fraction and tiny groups must be read alongside scores. Selected KMeans k maximizes within-space silhouette subject to minimum5% cluster size; all three choose k2. The small grid does not establish global algorithm optimality.
- Stability holds preprocessing fixed:20 n_init1 initialization stress tests versus production n_init20;20 paired80% refits on identical indices across spaces. ARI only shared households. Ward subsampling uses the selected KMeans k for controlled same-k comparison. There is no DBSCAN stability claim.
- At k2, RFM/Behavior/Product KMeans mean subsample ARI=.9797/.9700/.9639, Ward=.5351/.4574/.3135. k3 KMeans mean ARI=.9700/.7632/.9290. Empirical repeat ranges/quantiles are not confidence intervals for population stability.
- k2 future-spend eta²=.20375/.19711/.20185. Conditional paired1000 household-bootstrap Product-minus-RFM eta² difference=-.00190,95% percentile interval[-.01426,.00860]. At fixedk3 eta²=.31299/.21498/.17001; contrast=-.14298,CI[-.16518,-.11876]. These describe fixed-cluster spending stratification and do not measure prediction or causality. Bootstrap intervals do not account for full pipeline refitting; a single time split cannot establish generalization. Future-spend criterion is naturally related to RFM monetary intensity.
- Category coordinate multipliers .5/1/2 at k3 produce ARI .4328/1/.4597 against equal-block labels; corresponding category variance multipliers .25/1/4. Maximum-overlap alignment to 1× yields 620 switches for 0.5× and 585 for 2×. Flow bands count households on each adjacent transition. Strong sensitivity limits a unique-segmentation claim. Common-RFM scores are an RFM geometry diagnostic, not a neutral feature comparison.
- Selected Product k=2 profiles remain broad activity groups. Calibration deviations reach about 1.5 percentage points from the overall household mean.
- Maximum-overlap alignment of selected Product to RFM labels yields 287 changed assignments (11.5%). The lower-to-higher and higher-to-lower transitions contain 163 and 124 households. Within each transition, the household nearest its centroid in transformed Product space is the representative. IDs 2389 and 1970 are selected using calibration features alone. Cases illustrate assignment changes under the combined behavior and category representation.
- The exploratory later-category comparison uses the same 2,439 later-active households and the same calibration-defined eight departments plus OTHER in both periods. Each household contributes one normalized recorded-sales vector. The 59 later-inactive households remain in activity denominators and have undefined later composition. Category η² is the between-cluster fraction of squared Euclidean dispersion in the raw shares. RFM/Behavior/Product η² values are 0.002503/0.002206/0.005285 in calibration and 0.002992/0.001798/0.003860 in holdout. The paired 1,000-resample Product-minus-RFM holdout difference is +0.000868 with 95% conditional percentile interval [−0.001097, +0.003148]. The profiles show period-specific deviations from the active-cohort mean. The effect is small, the interval spans zero, and the holdout window was previously inspected for spending.
